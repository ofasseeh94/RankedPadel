Ranking

What is a NADD Level?

Your NADD Level is your padel skill level based on confirmed match results. It helps other players and clubs know what level of game fits you.

Example: If you consistently win close matches against stronger players, your score and confidence improve. If you lose to stronger players, your level may stay stable because the system understands the match context.

L

NADD Level

Your visible playing level. Used to match you with suitable games.

S

NADD Score

Your more detailed performance score behind the level.

C

Rank Confidence

How sure the system is about your level based on confirmed matches.

F

Fair Play

A separate behavior score for confirmations, no-shows, disputes, and reliability.

How NADD keeps rankings fair

Competitive players need to know the ranking is protected before they care about the score.

1

Opponent confirmation

A ranked result only counts after confirmation from the other team.

2

Dispute review

Mismatched scores can be reviewed before rank movement is final.

3

Pattern detection

Repeated abuse, fake scores, and abnormal win patterns are flagged.

4

Fair Play tracking

No-shows, late cancellations, and behavior issues are tracked separately from skill.

5

Confidence-based ranking

Your level becomes more reliable as you complete more confirmed matches.

Examples

What it looks like in real life

Simple journeys make the product easier to understand than product language alone.

P

Example player journey

Omar creates a profile and gets a provisional NADD Level. He records 3 confirmed ranked matches, his level becomes verified, and he can join better-matched games, compare himself on the city board, and share match receipts.

Example club journey

A club hosts a NADD Night for N5-N6 players. Players are matched by level, results are confirmed after each match, the club board updates, and open slots can be filled by players at the right level.

Example group session

A group opens Game Maker, adds names, runs Americano or Mexicano, and later decides whether confirmed results should count toward NADD Levels.

Can I use NADD with my existing group?

Yes. You can use Game Maker to run Americano, Mexicano, or tournament formats.

?

Does every match affect my level?

Only eligible ranked matches confirmed by the other team should affect your NADD Level.

?

Can people fake results?

Ranked results need confirmation from the other team. Suspicious patterns and disputes can be reviewed before they affect rankings.

Can clubs use this?

Yes. Clubs can run ranked nights, fill open slots, seed tournaments, and show club boards.
