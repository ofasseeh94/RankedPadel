Ranking

Your level is not what you say. It is what your matches prove.

NADD turns confirmed match results into a trusted level, score, confidence rating, and fair play record.

NADD Level

Your public competitive tier. Example: N4.3 Competitive.

NADD Score

The number behind your level. Visible to you and admins, not other players.

Rank Confidence

How sure the system is, based on volume, recency, and verified results.

Fair Play Score

Your reliability record for showing up, confirming fairly, and protecting the community.
